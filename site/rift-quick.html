FLEX: record and play back live

Eight buffers (B1-B8). Record a track, the input, the whole mix or a bus of tracks, in time, then play it like a sample.

SOURCETRK1-16 · IN · MAIN · BUS
REC TRIG
BUFFER B1-B8
FLEX TRACKa note trig plays it
  1. 1

    Put FLEX on a track

    [FUNC]+[SYN]

    MACHINE SEL → FLEX. Its trig length becomes INF so you hear whole takes. BUF picks the buffer it plays.

  2. 2

    Open FLEX REC

    [FUNC]+[VOICE]

    On the FLEX track. LEFT / RIGHT: REC TRIGS ↔ REC SETUP ↔ REC BUS.

  3. 3

    Choose what to record

    REC SETUP

    SRC: a track (TRK1-16), IN, MAIN or BUS. R.LEN: how many steps. BUF: which buffer.

  4. 4

    Place recorder trigs

    [TRIG]or[FUNC]+[TRIG]

    On REC TRIGS: [TRIG] a one-shot (records once), [FUNC] + [TRIG] every pass. [PAGE] for more steps.

  5. 5

    Play and listen

    [PLAY]

    The take records on its step. A FLEX note trig on that step plays it as it arrives.

  • Record now, no trigs[YES] while playing: Q.REC waits for the next boundary, then records R.LEN steps. [NO] cancels.
  • Re-arm one-shots[YES] [YES]
  • Keep a take[FUNC] + [YES]: next empty sample slot, onto the +Drive.
  • Clear a buffer[FUNC] + [NO] on REC SETUP, then [YES].
  • Free the CPUMUTE SRC ON: the source is silent while the take plays.

SMEAR: granular looper

One loop of a sample (or a FLEX buffer), read two ways: PLAY like tape, GRN as a cloud of grains. BLND mixes them.

  • GrainsDENS how many · OVLP how long · SPRY how scattered · PRND detune · WIN shape
  • BLND0: PLAY (tape) → 127: GRN (grains)
  • FRZHolds the read point; PLAY's share turns into grains
  1. 1

    Load a sample

    SAMP

    Turn SAMP: the sample list opens. [YES] keeps it. Past 127 are the FLEX buffers.

  2. 2

    Play it as tape

    BLND 0

    Notes and TUNE transpose it. SPD changes speed (and pitch); its centre stops the read point.

  3. 3

    Pick the loop

    STRTSIZE

    Small SIZE loops a tiny piece. Move STRT, or put an LFO on it, to scan.

  4. 4

    Turn it into grains

    BLND → 127

    DENS for more grains, OVLP for longer ones, SPRY to scatter, PRND to detune, WIN to shape.

  5. 5

    Freeze the moment

    FRZ ON

    Page 2, knob E. The read point holds and keeps sounding as grains while you play over it.

The phase vocoder (VOC) is switched off for now: BLND's VOC end plays PLAY. It returns once it fits the DSP.

SILT

Material synthesis: strings, grains, rubber and molten fluid, played by ten gestures, alone or as a crowd.

Fastest soundStart on page 4: MAT (what it is), GEST (what you do), MODE (one player or many). Then PRESS (how hard) and SPEED. Velocity and the trig's length play the gesture.

  • Stable to squealraise PRESS × ROSIN
  • ClusterSPRD × DRIFT × COHE in MASS
  • Water to honeyFLUID · VISC × TENS × SPEED
  • Pluck to thudSKIN · SNAP × VISC

LIGATURE

A patchable block synth: twelve small blocks wired together, 128 patches per project.

Fastest soundPick a factory PATCH (1-13) and play. To rewire, press [YES] on page 1.

  • EDIT ([YES])A block · B type · C/D input A + amount · E/F input B + amount · G B-moves-a-setting · H output block
  • Empty a block[FUNC] + [NO]
  • Pages 2-4Two settings a block (1RISE, 3CUT…), offsets: 0 is the patch as wired
  • A voice to builde.g. PHAS into WAVE, into LP (a SLOP on its B), into MULT (another SLOP on its B), OUT on the MULT

CONTOUR

Wave terrain synthesis: a point orbits over a surface; the height under it is the sound.

Fastest soundSURF SINE with small SIZX/SIZY is a clean tone. Open the sizes for brightness, move CNTX/CNTY to find a spot, then try SURF and RATIO.

GLASS filter and the plate

GLASS: a ringing filter on any machine's FLTR page (pick Glass after Equalizer). The master REVERB is a plate now.

Fastest soundGLASS: raise RES, set FREQ to the pitch you want it to ring at, hit it with short sounds. KEY.T up: the ring follows the notes. Page 2's LPG (from 64): a bongo-like ping.

  • LPGPage 2, in ENV DELAY's place: from 64, level and cutoff open on each trig and close like a vactrol (DEC sets how long).
  • KEY.THow far the cutoff follows the note.

Tips

Any machine

[TRK] + CLEAR ([FUNC] + [PLAY]) resets a RIFT machine to its own start sound.

Any machine

Hold a trig to see its p-locks highlighted with their values.

Any machine

Word controls move four detents a choice: they never glide, even under LFOs.

FASCIA

[FUNC] + turn: TUNE jumps octaves; GEOM and SHIFT snap to 0.

CPU

When the DSP is busy, the oldest tails drop first, never the newest note or a held chord.

CPU

SILT's QUAL DEEP costs the most DSP time: use it on one or two tracks.

FLEX

Record a CPU-heavy track into a buffer, play it from FLEX, mute the source (MUTE SRC).

FLEX

Any sample machine can play a buffer: turn SAMP past 127 to B1-B8.

FLEX

A loud BUS or MAIN take can clip: turn GAIN down.

SMEAR

An LFO on SPD, STRT or BLND gives it motion: STRT scans, BLND moves between tape and cloud.

SMEAR

p-lock FRZ ON on single trigs for frozen stutters.

Slides

TRIG page 2: SLD on, SLEN sets the time; SLEN NXT glides until the next trig.

Direct Jump

[PTN] + [YES] on: a pattern cued with [PTN] + [TRIG] starts at once, from the same position. [PTN] + [NO] off.

Keyboard

[PTN] + [KEYBOARD]: KEYBOARD HOLD keeps notes on; again releases them.

Tuning

KB SETUP's H is TUNE: turn it for the track's tuning, push it for the TUNING page. SLICE and GRID are never retuned.

GLASS

Max RES + KEY.T up: GLASS becomes a playable resonator for short hits.

Everything else (p-locks, LFOs, kits, MIDI) works as on the stock Digitone II. Unofficial: not made or supported by Elektron. Full manual.
