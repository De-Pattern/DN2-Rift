# ⚠ v0.16 / v0.17: don't play SMEAR or PLATEAU yet

**SMEAR (v0.16, v0.17) and PLATEAU (v0.17) can silence the whole Digitone until you reboot.** A SMEAR note gives a short burst, then the output goes silent, and every other machine with it.

- **For now, don't put SMEAR or PLATEAU on a track.**
- **If your output has died:** power-cycle the Digitone. Everything comes back, and your projects are unharmed.
- **Power-cycle once after flashing** RIFT, before you play: the first start after a flash can leave ORGAN silent.

The cause is being fixed: a guard on every RIFT machine's output, so one machine can never mute the unit again, plus the fault itself. The gate gets checks for both. The fix comes in **v0.18**, with GLASS.

Thanks to everyone who reported it.
