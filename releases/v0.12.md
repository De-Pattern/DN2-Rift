# v0.12 · PATCHING

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.12 makes **LIGATURE** a real patcher: you wire its twelve blocks yourself, on its own screen.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE and DIGIDRUM tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.12**
- **Page 1 shows the patch**: twelve tiles in three rows (1–9, A, B, C) with the cables between them (a cord back to an earlier block is dotted), and PATCH, TUNE, FINE, LFO1 / LFO2, VSNS, DRIV, LEVL below, each in its knob's place.
- **YES opens EDIT.** The knobs edit the wiring: A the block, B its type, C and D input A and its amount, E and F input B and its amount, G B MOVES, H the output block. The arrows move between tiles, FUNC + NO empties a block, YES shows the BLOCK view (what feeds it, its two settings by name, where it goes), NO leaves. Every change plays at once.
- **Your wirings are kept with the project**: 128 of them, saved two seconds after you stop editing (or when you leave EDIT), read back on LOAD and at power-on. PATCH (1–128) picks one, and can be p-locked. A new project starts with the old built-in patches, so LIGATURE tracks from earlier versions sound exactly as before.
- **Pages 2–4**: each knob is named for its block's setting (1RISE, 3CUT, 4GAIN…), and the names follow the block types.
- Includes v0.11's fix for held notes on a busy track.

**What to test**
1. A LIGATURE track, page 1: the grid shows the patch; turn PATCH and the grid changes.
2. YES: change a block's type (B), its inputs (C–F) and the output (H): the sound follows at once.
3. YES on a block: the BLOCK view; YES back; FUNC + NO empties the block; NO leaves.
4. Save the project, load another, load it back, and power-cycle: your wiring is still there.
5. A new project: the wirings are the old patches again.
6. Pages 2–4: the knob names match the blocks.
