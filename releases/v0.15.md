# v0.15 · THOLET

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.15 adds **THOLET**, a new piano machine with a grain cloud, and brings over the DN2 mk2 mod's **KEYBOARD HOLD**.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and every control, now in depth.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM and THOLET tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.15**
- **THOLET** (MACHINE SEL, after DIGIDRUM). **THOLET is Error Kit's concept.** It's a piano that leans toward a Rhodes:
  - one of six real Kawai grand hammer transients starts each note (HAMR);
  - two detuned sets of string partials ring under the hammer (TONE, DEC, STRK);
  - FELT gives a soft felt-piano sound, after Nils Frahm;
  - EP morphs the sound toward an electric piano's tine and bark.
- **THOLET's grain cloud** is on page 2. The note breaks up into grains as it fades: GRAN sets how much, and GDEN, GSIZ, GFM and GSPR shape the cloud. REL is the damper when the note ends.
- **KEYBOARD HOLD**, from the DN2 mk2 mod: hold **PTN** and press **KEYBOARD** to toggle KB HOLD ON/OFF. It is the stock sustain on all 16 tracks:
  - notes you're already holding stay on when you let go;
  - MIDI notes in and the arpeggiator's keys are held too;
  - HOLD OFF releases them all;
  - the sequencer's notes are not held.
- **The manual is rewritten in depth.** Every control on every machine now has its own entry. **LIGATURE** gets a full guide: the PATCH screen and EDIT, every source, all 22 blocks (what each does, its two settings and its two inputs), the factory patches and worked examples. Also covered: FLEX, the recorder and MON, microtuning, Direct Jump and slides.
- Includes v0.14 (FLEX MAIN/IN make-up, MON, MICROTUNING).

**What to test**
1. MACHINE SEL: THOLET after DIGIDRUM. Play it: a piano with a hammer's strike. Turn HAMR through its six hammers.
2. FELT up for the felt piano; EP up toward a Rhodes; STRK for warmth.
3. Page 2: GRAN up, hold a note and let it ring: the tail turns into grains. Shorter trigs: REL damps the note.
4. PTN + KEYBOARD: KB HOLD ON. In KEYBOARD mode, play and release a chord: it keeps sounding. Play notes from a MIDI keyboard: they hold too. PTN + KEYBOARD again: all released.
5. With HOLD on, the arpeggiator keeps playing the keys you let go.

**Known issues**
- A MAIN or IN FLEX buffer saved to a sample slot plays 12 dB quieter than the buffer did.
- ERASE MOD DATA doesn't clear the tunings yet.

**Coming next (not available yet)**

- **Eight new LIGATURE blocks** from WEFT (with SMEAR): TICK (a clock at a division of the tempo, with CHANCE), ENV, VCA, CDIV (clock divider), DUST, CHAOS, CRUSH and LISSA (a Lissajous LFO).
- **Machine logos in MACHINE SEL:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM (DRUM), FLEX (REC) and the DT2 sample machines (SAMP).
