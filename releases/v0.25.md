# v0.25 · SPALL

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.25 brings SPALL, a new synth machine built from tiny sound particles, and gives the MIDI machine its own logo.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, SPALL), RUBBER and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.25**
- **SPALL**, a new synth machine (MACHINE SEL, after CONTOUR): sound made of tiny events (clicks, dust, tonal grains, chips) fired by a stochastic clock and rung through two resonators. DENS and GAP set how busy it is, CLMP throws them in bursts, MEM gives the clock a memory; FRQA, FRQB, RING and ALG shape the resonators. Two pages, 16 controls, its own icons and logo.
- **The MIDI machine** has a 5-pin DIN logo in MACHINE SEL, and its label reads MIDI.
- **Download PDF** buttons on the manual and the quick guide.
- Includes v0.24 (RUBBER, SMEAR's pitch and speed apart).

**Known issue:** RUBBER can leave a track silent after a power-on (leftover memory in its filter). The fix is in v0.26, coming next. Until then, try another filter and RUBBER again after a fresh power-on.

**What to test**
1. A SPALL note at the defaults, then each TYPE.
2. DENS, GAP and CLMP: from a few clicks to a storm.
3. FRQA / FRQB and RING: tune the resonators to the note.

**Coming next (not available yet)**

- Then FASCIA and the plate made cheaper, the Monomachine chorus with pre-sliced FLEX buffers, LIGATURE's Leibniz blocks, SPALL's second stage.
