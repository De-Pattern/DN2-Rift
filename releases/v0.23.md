# v0.23 · STEADY

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.23 goes after FASCIA's crackle and shows you when the DSP runs out of time.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR), GLASS and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.23**
- **FASCIA's crackle (v0.21, even with one note):** the DSP's time guard was too cautious with FASCIA, RIFT's heaviest voice, and with the new plate reverb's few extra cycles it dropped a lone voice on some blocks, which clicks. It now budgets FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM and CONTOUR more tightly; the safety reserve at the end of the DSP's frame is unchanged.
- **CPU on RIFT's machine pages:** small letters C-P-U appear at the right edge of a RIFT machine's page while the DSP has just had to leave a voice out for time. A click with CPU showing is the DSP running out of time; a click without it is something else: please tell us.
- **CONTOUR's new logo** in MACHINE SEL.
- **A quick guide** to every machine and its controls, beside the manual.
- Includes v0.22.

**What to test**
1. FASCIA, one note, then busy patterns: no clicks.
2. If anything clicks, is CPU showing?
3. MACHINE SEL: CONTOUR's logo.
