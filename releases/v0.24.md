# v0.24 · APART

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.24 brings RUBBER, a new SH-101-style filter in GLASS's place, and gives SMEAR the Nebulae's trick: pitch and speed apart.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR), RUBBER and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.24**
- **RUBBER**, a filter type after the Roland SH-101's, on every machine's FLTR page, in GLASS's place: a four-pole ladder with the chip's soft saturation, snappy and rubbery, made for mono basslines. RES goes all the way to self-oscillation. **DRIV** (in TYPE's place) drives it from clean to thick. Its envelope's curves, **ABND** and **DBND**, are knobs on FLTR page 2; SUS and REL are blank on page 1. Sounds saved with GLASS open with RUBBER.
- **SMEAR: pitch and speed apart**, as on the Nebulae. TUNE and the note change the pitch only; SPD changes the speed only. Untransposed at speed 1, it plays exactly as before.
- **Cleaner knob icons** on RIFT's machine pages.
- Includes v0.23.

**What to test**

2. RUBBER's FLTR page 2: ABND and DBND knobs.
3. SMEAR: TUNE changes pitch without changing speed.
