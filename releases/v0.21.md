# v0.21 · PLATE

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.21 puts a plate in place of the master reverb, brings a new start-up animation and the DN2 mk2 mod's FLEX levels, and withdraws PLATEAU for rework.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR) and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.21**
- **The master reverb is a plate**: Dattorro's figure-eight tank (PLATEAU's sound) in stereo, behind the stock REVERB page. The page and its controls are unchanged, so sends, routing, LFO destinations and projects work as before:
  - **PRE**: pre-delay, up to 0.3 s. **DEC**: from a short room to a long plate.
  - **FREQ** and **GAIN** (stock's feedback shelving): a high shelf inside the tank. FREQ its corner, GAIN how much brightness survives each pass.
  - HPF, LPF, MIX VOL and routing are stock's.
- **A new start-up animation:** the smoke gathers into RIFT in block letters, which then drip and decompose. The skeletons have rounder skulls and joints, and the drums are proper djembes and a steel pan.
- **PLATEAU is withdrawn** while it is reworked (it glitched with input gain and did not flow with the trigs): out of MACHINE SEL, and a saved PLATEAU track opens as **FM Tone**.
- **FLEX record levels** from the DN2 mk2 mod's v0.71 (NiKo's measurements): a take of a track, of MAIN or of IN plays back at its source's level at GAIN 0. Takes recorded on earlier versions keep their level (a track's take is 18 dB hot: GAIN -18 brings it back).
- Includes v0.20 (SLEN NXT, CONTOUR's FDBK rescaled and in key on high notes).

**What to test**
1. The master reverb: a track's reverb send up; PRE, DEC, FREQ and GAIN round their ranges. Sends at 0: no reverb at all.
2. Power on: the new logo forms, drips and crumbles; "RIFT 0.21" bottom right.
3. MACHINE SEL: no PLATEAU; everything else where it was.
4. FLEX: record a track, MAIN and IN at GAIN 0: each take plays at its source's level.
