# v0.4 · ORGAN

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.4 adds the second one, **ORGAN**, and fixes FASCIA's starting sound and its clear.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA and ORGAN tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.4**
- **ORGAN** (MACHINE SEL, after FASCIA): the pipe organ from the Ravel firmware, at 48 kHz. Five stops (FLUTE, NACHTHORN, PRINCIPAL, GAMBA, TRUMPET) whose pipes change with pitch as real ranks do; seven drawbar ranks (16', 8', 4', Nazard, 2', Tierce, 1'); chiff, speech, spread, air and an unsteady wind; a tremulant and a celeste. Two pages, every control p-lockable, an LFO destination and slidable. It costs much less DSP than a FASCIA voice. An organ holds until the next note on its track, so shape it with the AMP envelope (set a release).
- **FASCIA starts as the demo vibraphone**, with CHAR back at 0 (127 drove every sound into its saturator).
- **TRK + CLEAR** on a FASCIA or ORGAN track restores that machine's own defaults, not stock's (HARD 100, DUST 64, TUNE +36...).
- **FUNC + turn on GEOM and SHIFT** stops at 0 on the way through the middle.

**What to test**
1. ORGAN from MACHINE SEL: a held note sounds an organ at once; each STOP; each rank alone; 16' on a low note.
2. CHIFF and SPCH on fast notes; SPRD high (the ranks enter one by one); TREM with RATE; CELST against the 8'.
3. Chords and fast arpeggios on ORGAN: no click on a retrigger.
4. FASCIA: a new track is the vibraphone; turn knobs, then TRK + CLEAR brings the vibraphone back.
5. Save and load a project with ORGAN and FASCIA tracks.

**Good to know**
- A FASCIA patch with BEND up and DAMP near 0 can sustain on some hits and decay short on others, and now and then a voice cuts out. Keep BEND at 10 or less (or raise DAMP) until the fix lands.
- The version shows at the top of the sample browser (RIFT v0.4).
