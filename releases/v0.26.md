# v0.26 · VOC

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.26 brings SMEAR's VOC back, rebuilt on heavylight's keyframe time stretch.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, SPALL), RUBBER and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.26**
- **CONTOUR's FDBK is back to its original range**, exactly as it first was: the curve added in v0.20 is gone.
- **SMEAR's VOC is back.** BLND 0 now plays a keyframe time stretch: SPD changes time without changing pitch, TUNE and the keyboard change pitch without changing time, and SPD 64 or FRZ holds a moment. It splices peak onto peak with short fades, and costs a small fraction of what the old phase vocoder did.
- **Thanks to heavylight**: the stretch follows his keyframe time stretching (Capicola and dt2-keyframer, heavylight-industries, and the DAFx26 paper *Keyframe Time Stretching via Extrema Sampling*), rewritten for SMEAR with his permission.
- BLND: VOC at 0, PLAY in the middle, grains at 127.
- **RUBBER: the silent track is fixed.** A track could go silent for good when RUBBER was put on it (leftover memory in its filter state at power-on). It now starts clean.
- Includes v0.25 (SPALL) and v0.24 (RUBBER).

**What to test**
0. CONTOUR: FDBK with FM and the ratios. If a sound breaks up, watch for CPU at the right of the screen: that is the DSP's time running out, not the feedback.
1. SMEAR at BLND 0: TUNE moves the pitch only, SPD the time only.
2. Slow SPD on drums and voices: do the splices click?
3. SPD 64 or FRZ: a held, looping moment.
4. RUBBER on a track straight after power-on: it sounds.
