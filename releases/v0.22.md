# v0.22 · FROST

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.22 fixes GLASS's overload sound, makes SMEAR's TUNE work and gives it a FREEZE control and a roomier page 2, and gives CONTOUR its own logo.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR), GLASS and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.22**
- **GLASS: the crushed, overloaded sound is fixed.** GLASS on several busy tracks could run the DSP past its frame, which sounds like the Monomachine's overload. It now does exactly the same arithmetic (the same sound, bit for bit) at well under two thirds of the DSP time, and its time counts toward the DSP's budget, so a busy DSP lets the oldest tails go instead of running over.
- **SMEAR: TUNE works.** TUNE and the note now move PLAY, tape-style (higher plays faster). Before, at BLND 0 they did nothing.
- **SMEAR: FRZ (Freeze)**, page 2's knob E. ON holds the read point and turns PLAY's share into grains, so the frozen moment keeps sounding (DENS, OVLP, SPRY, PRND, WIN shape it). OFF moves on at SPD. p-lock it for stutters. Tracks saved before v0.22 open with FRZ off.
- **SMEAR: page 2 redrawn** like ONESHOT's: the sample across the screen, and small pictures of what OVLP, SPRY, PRND and WIN do above it.
- **CONTOUR has its own MACHINE SEL logo:** a terrain's contour lines.
- **The manual** is up to date (KEYBOARD HOLD back, the FLEX levels as they are, SMEAR's VOC status said plainly).
- Includes v0.21 (the plate master reverb, the new start-up logo, the FLEX levels).

**About SMEAR's phase vocoder:** VOC is still off, as it has been since v0.18: it needed more DSP time than a frame has and a VOC note silenced the unit. A cheaper stretch is being designed to bring it back.

**What to test**
1. GLASS on six or more busy tracks (high RES, ENV, LPG), with FASCIA or SMEAR beside them: no crushed, crackly sound. GLASS's own sound is unchanged.
2. SMEAR at BLND 0: TUNE and the keyboard transpose it.
3. SMEAR FRZ: ON holds the sound as a grain cloud while notes play over it; OFF moves on. A p-locked FRZ on single trigs.
4. SMEAR page 2: the sample view clear of the knobs; the four pictures move with their knobs; FRZ shows a bar when on.
5. MACHINE SEL: CONTOUR shows its contour-lines logo.
