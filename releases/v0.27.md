# v0.27 · FLOW

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.27 makes it run smoother under load and brings over DN2 mk2's latest fixes and the arp additions.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, SPALL), RUBBER and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.27**
- **The CPU marker no longer stands for nothing.** On SPALL and ORGAN it showed most of the time with nothing wrong. The DSP's time guard asked every track for a large reserve on every block; it now expects a voice to cost what it cost on the block before, and asks for the reserve only on the block that starts a note. Tracks are left out far less often when many play.
- **CONTOUR plays several voices with FDBK up.** The guard reserved the cost of the highest notes for every CONTOUR voice with feedback, all the time, so a second voice seldom fitted. It now reserves by the note, and only as the note starts. (Above B4 with feedback a voice still costs about three times a low one: that is the sound's own work.)
- **From DN2 mk2:** live recording no longer lags (v0.74); a FLEX take follows its source track's LEVEL (v0.75).
- **The arpeggiator on MIDI tracks** (Angel Linares's, from upstream).
- **ARP OFS IN SCALE** (SETTINGS > PERSONALIZE): arp offsets count scale degrees. **In-key transpose**: [KEYBOARD] + [FUNC] + [+]/[-] moves the track by scale degrees. Both are w-winter's, from upstream.
- Nothing about any machine's sound changed: every machine is still held bit for bit to its model.
- Includes v0.26 (SMEAR's VOC, CONTOUR's FDBK as it first was, RUBBER's silent track).

**What to test**
1. SPALL and ORGAN alone: no CPU at the right of the screen.
2. Two or three CONTOUR tracks with FDBK up, low and high notes.
3. A busy project: does anything drop out that did not before? Hold FUNC on a machine's page for the DSP's load.
4. Live recording: no lag. A FLEX take at a lowered track LEVEL.
5. The arp on a MIDI track; ARP OFS IN SCALE; in-key transpose.
