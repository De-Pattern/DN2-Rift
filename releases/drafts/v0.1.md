# v0.1 · FASCIA

The first RIFT build. RIFT is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod: no Machinedrum or Monomachine machines, and new engines in their place, starting with FASCIA.

**Build it:** the RIFT Patcher builds it in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files (no Monomachine file needed any more), and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**New in RIFT**
- **FASCIA**, a physical and spectral percussion and tuned-body machine, exactly the sound from the approved demo tour. Two exciters (STRIKE, BLOW, BOW, DUST, PULSE, TZFM) drive two coupled modal bodies (membrane, plate, bar, vibe, chime, bell, glass, tube, string), with thru-zero FM, ring mod and frequency shift, a spectral morph, formants, water, folding, drive and a low-pass gate.
- **32 controls on four pages:** SYN1 EXCITERS, SYN2 RESONATORS, SYN3 SPECTRAL, SYN4 ONSET/INTERACT. All of them p-lockable and slid; all but ATK, FDBK and VARY can be LFO destinations.
- **Kept from the DN2 mk2 mod:** the Digitakt II sample machines (Oneshot, Werp, Stretch, Repitch, Grid, Slice, Flex), the +Drive sample library and per-project pools, the recorder and resampling, Direct Jump and parameter slides.
- **Stock start-up screen** for now; RIFT gets its own loader later. The version shows at the top of the sample browser (RIFT v0.1).

**Good to know**
- FASCIA is heavy. In the emulator, about 3 voices of the heaviest patches (water, snare wires) fit in the DSP's time at once, about 6 of a typical one and 8 of the lightest (kalimba), with nothing else playing. When it runs out of time a voice drops out for that moment, and sample voices always get their time first. The unit's DSP LOAD will tell us the real numbers.
- A DN2 mk2 project with a Perc or Monomachine track opens that track as FM Tone, as stock does.
- OS 1.11 only (not 1.12). Back up your +Drive and projects first.

**What to test**
1. FASCIA from MACHINE SEL: all four pages, the knobs and the choice boxes.
2. The audition sounds: glass, bells, drums, the bowed and blown ones, water.
3. P-locks, slides and LFOs on FASCIA's controls.
4. Chords and fast notes: how many voices hold before one drops, and whether a drop sounds clean.
5. FASCIA beside sample tracks: the samples should never crackle.
6. Save and load a project with FASCIA tracks; presets and sound locks.
