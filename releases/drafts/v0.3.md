# v0.3 · FLEX

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place, starting with **FASCIA**. This is its first public release (v0.1 was the first cleared build; v0.3 contains it).

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**In RIFT**
- **FASCIA:** a physical and spectral percussion and tuned-body machine. Two exciters (STRIKE, BLOW, BOW, DUST, PULSE, TZFM) drive two coupled modal bodies (membrane, plate, bar, vibe, chime, bell, glass, tube, string), with thru-zero FM, ring mod and frequency shift, a spectral morph, formants, water, folding, drive and a low-pass gate. 32 controls on four pages (SYN1 EXCITERS, SYN2 RESONATORS, SYN3 SPECTRAL, SYN4 ONSET/INTERACT), all p-lockable and slid.
- **Kept from the DN2 mk2 mod:** the Digitakt II sample machines (Oneshot, Werp, Stretch, Repitch, Grid, Slice, Flex), the +Drive library and project pools, the recorder and resampling, Direct Jump and parameter slides.

**New in v0.3**
- **FLEX as in the mod's v0.53:** MUTE SRC (the source track leaves the mix while FLEX REC records it), R.LEN at the pattern's scale, a voice plays its own locked BUF, R.LEN a bar a detent with FUNC, PLAY two detents, INF trig length on a switch to FLEX.
- **FASCIA starts at DAMP 28 and CHAR 127**, so a new FASCIA track rings out and is heard right away. Saved sounds keep their own values.
- **FUNC + turn on FASCIA's TUNE jumps by octaves** (−2 to +2), as the mod's tunes do.

**What to test**
1. FASCIA from MACHINE SEL: all four pages, the knobs and choice boxes; a new track rings long (DAMP 28, CHAR 127).
2. FUNC + TUNE: each click lands on the next octave.
3. P-locks, slides and LFOs on FASCIA's controls; chords and fast notes (how many voices hold before one drops).
4. FASCIA beside sample tracks: the samples should never crackle.
5. FLEX REC with MUTE SRC on and off; a BUF lock on a playback trig.
6. Save and load a project with FASCIA and FLEX tracks; presets and sound locks.

**Good to know**
- FASCIA is heavy: about 3 voices of the heaviest patches and 6 to 8 of typical ones fit at once. When it runs out of DSP time a voice drops out for that moment, and sample voices always get their time first.
- The version shows at the top of the sample browser (RIFT v0.3).
