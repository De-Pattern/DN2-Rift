# v0.5 · BREATH

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.5 gives **ORGAN** two new pages: **ATTACK** and **BREATH**.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA and ORGAN tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.5**
- **ORGAN SYN3 · ATTACK:** PERC (a percussion ping at each note), PDEC (its decay), PHRM (ping on the 2nd or 3rd harmonic), KCLK (key click), VELO (velocity sensitivity), MIXT (a mixture: high pipes above the stop), ROTR and RSPD (a rotor, its speed easing between settings).
- **ORGAN SYN4 · BREATH:** BRTH (turbulent air inside the tone), TURB (its speed, a slow waver to a rasp), BREK and BDEP (a voice-like break: the tone cracks down into a half-pitch growl and recovers), PRES (blowing pressure), BLOM (each note blooms from breath into tone), EDGE (breath heard through the pipe), PVEL (velocity on pressure).
- Everything on the new pages starts at 0, and at 0 ORGAN sounds exactly as in v0.4. All are p-lockable and slide; all but BLOM, EDGE and PVEL are LFO destinations.
- **ORGAN sounds saved in v0.4 sound as before.** Their pages 3 and 4 show leftover numbers until you turn one of those knobs; then both pages take their defaults.
- **CELESTE** fades its rank in and out instead of switching it (no click going 0 → 2 and back).

**What to test**
1. A v0.4 ORGAN sound: it sounds as before; turn PERC and the new pages read their defaults.
2. PERC on fast notes; PDEC short to long; PHRM 2ND and 3RD; KCLK; VELO soft and hard.
3. MIXT; ROTR with RSPD slow then fast.
4. BRTH with TURB; BREK and BDEP; PRES; BLOM on long notes; EDGE.
5. CELST 0 → 2 and back on a held note: no click.
6. Save, power-cycle and reload a project with the new pages set.
