# v0.6 · SILT

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.6 adds **SILT**, RIFT's third machine, and changes three **ORGAN** controls that Error Kit asked for.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. SILT is brand new and is the heaviest engine on the DSP. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN and SILT tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.6**
- **SILT** (MACHINE SEL: Silt, after Organ): play a material with a gesture.
  - **MAT:** FIBER (string), SILT (grains and dust), SKIN (rubber), FLUID (a molten body that drips).
  - **GEST:** BOW, SCRAPE, STACC, TREMO, SPICC, LEGNO, GLISS, FRACT, SWELL, QUAKE.
  - **MODE:** SOLO, MASS (an ensemble in pitch), MASS-8 (an octave down), SWARM.
  - Pages MATTER, CONTACT and STRUCTURE shape it; CONFIG (page 4) picks the material and gesture, and can be p-locked per trig.
  - Velocity strikes harder and bows faster (in MASS it also brings in more players). The gate length is how long the gesture lasts. LOOP repeats it while the gate is held.
  - QUAL sets the DSP cost (ECO, NORMAL, DEEP). Use ECO for big chords.
- **ORGAN:**
  - **BLOM** now opens the pipe slowly into the note, a clean swell for drones. The breath as it opens comes from **EDGE**, so EDGE at 0 gives no breath.
  - **MTUN** (Mixture Tune) replaces PHRM: it tunes the mixture down by 0 to 36 semitones.
  - **MCHR** (Mixture Character) replaces VELO: it morphs the mixture from glassy toward a buzzy trumpet pipe. PVEL still puts velocity on the pressure.
  - v0.5 organ sounds with MIXT up will sound different (their old PHRM and VELO values now read as MTUN and MCHR). Set MTUN and MCHR to 0 for the plain mixture.

**Known issues**
- SILT's materials aren't level-matched yet: FIBER is about twice as loud as the others.

**What to test**
1. SILT's init sound (a bowed string) with short and long notes.
2. Every MAT, GEST and MODE; velocity soft and hard in MASS.
3. LOOP on a long note; p-lock MAT or GEST on single trigs.
4. Several SILT tracks at DEEP with chords: listen for dropouts; then try ECO.
5. ORGAN: BLOM up with EDGE at 0, then EDGE up; MIXT with MTUN and MCHR.
6. Save, power-cycle and reload a project with SILT tracks.
