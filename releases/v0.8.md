# v0.8 · RESONATOR

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.8 brings back **RESONATOR**, the Rings-style modal resonator from the DN2 mk2 mod's early versions, as RIFT's fourth machine.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT and RESONATOR tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.8**
- **RESONATOR** (MACHINE SEL: Resonator, after Silt): 24 resonating modes a voice, struck by a mallet or a burst of noise. One page: TUNE (semitones), LEV, STRC (string, then marimba bar, then bell), BRIT, DAMP (0.1 to 12 seconds), POS (where it is struck), EXCT (mallet to noise), HIT.
- Every control p-locks, slides and takes LFOs. Notes ring through retriggers, and a voice that has rung out costs nothing.

**What to test**
1. Put RESONATOR on a track and play: each note rings at its pitch.
2. Sweep STRC from 0 to 127 while playing.
3. Long DAMP with chords; several RESONATOR tracks at once: listen for dropouts.
4. P-lock STRC and DAMP on single trigs; an LFO on POS.
5. Save, power-cycle and reload a project with RESONATOR tracks.
