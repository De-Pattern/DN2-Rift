# v0.7 · FLEXPOOL

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.7 brings over the newest FLEX from the DN2 mk2 mod, the Digitone's own NAME screen, an ORGAN fix, and a new start-up animation.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, section 6 (RIFT), at the bottom of the page: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN and SILT tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.7**
- **FLEX buffers in every sample machine** (from the mod's v0.55): SAMP on ONESHOT, WERP, STRETCH, REPITCH, GRID and SLICE turns past 127 onto **B1..B8**, the eight FLEX buffers. A track on a buffer plays its latest take; SAMP p-locks can pick a buffer.
- **FLEX MUTE SRC** (the mod's v0.57): the source is muted only while the FLEX track plays it.
- **The Digitone's own NAME screen** (the mod's v0.54) for FILE NAME, RENAME and CREATE FOLDER.
- **Values on RIFT's own machines:** on FASCIA, ORGAN and SILT, turning a knob now shows its value where its name was, for a second, as stock does. With a p-locked trig held, it shows that trig's lock.
- **ORGAN:** BLOM now shapes the attack of the breath (the blown wind), not the whole instrument (Error Kit).
- **A new start-up animation:** robots drumming around a campfire, and the smoke spells RIFT.

**Known issues**
- LIGATURE (the block-patch machine) is built but held back from MACHINE SEL until its own block-and-cable screens are ready.

**What to test**
1. Record a take with FLEX, then on a ONESHOT track turn SAMP past 127 to B1: it plays the take.
2. P-lock SAMP to B2 on a single trig.
3. MUTE SRC: the source goes quiet only while the FLEX track plays.
4. Rename a sample and create a folder: the stock NAME screen opens and keeps the name.
5. ORGAN with BLOM up: the breath (the wind noise) swells in; the tone itself is not delayed.
6. Hold a p-locked trig on FASCIA, ORGAN or SILT and turn a knob: its value (the lock) shows under it.
7. Power on: the campfire plays, then RIFT 0.7.
