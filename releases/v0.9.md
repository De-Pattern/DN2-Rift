# v0.9 · LINE

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.9 gives every RIFT machine a new screen style, **LINE**.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT and LIGATURE tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.9**
- **LIGATURE is in MACHINE SEL**, on the new LINE pages.
- **RESONATOR is removed.** A RESONATOR track from v0.8 opens as FM Tone in v0.9, so its sound is not kept: save a copy of any project that uses it before you update.
- **LINE**, a new screen for FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT and LIGATURE: thin lines and one small icon per control, in two rows of four that sit where the knobs are. Each icon moves as you turn its knob.
- **No side panel:** the whole width goes to the controls, and nothing overlaps.
- **Values show the Syntakt way:** when you turn a knob, while you hold SYN, or while you hold a trig.
- **P-locks show up:** hold a trig and its locked controls are highlighted, each with its locked value.
- **Word choices sit in boxes**, and they scroll when they change.
- **A manual:** the MANUAL button beside the version list on the patcher opens a short guide to installing, recovering, and every RIFT machine's pages.
- The other sound engines are unchanged from v0.8.

**What to test**
1. Open each RIFT machine's pages: nothing overlaps or is cut off, and the track number reads cleanly.
2. Turn every knob: its icon moves and its value shows, then hides again.
3. Hold SYN: every value shows.
4. P-lock a few controls, then hold that trig: the locked cells are highlighted, with their values.
5. Turn a word-choice control: its box scrolls to the new word.
6. LIGATURE: pick it in MACHINE SEL, play it, and go through its pages.

**Coming next (not available yet)**
- **DIGIDRUM:** digital drum voices after the Syntakt's (BD Modern, SD Basic, SD Vintage, CP Vintage, CY Alloy, PC Carbon), with a TYPE you can p-lock per trig.
- **THOLET:** a grand piano that leans toward a Rhodes, with short sampled hammer transients, a felt layer after Nils Frahm, and a grain cloud the note can break up into. **THOLET is Error Kit's concept.**
- **A granular, phase-vocoded stretchy looper** after the Qu-Bit Nebulae, taking WERP's place among the sample machines.
- **FX machines:** a track type that other tracks feed, starting with clones of the Tonverk's frequency shifter and Chrono Pitch.
- **A stretch algorithm of our own.**
