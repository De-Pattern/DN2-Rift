# v0.18 · GLASS

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.18 **fixes the silent-unit bug** (a SMEAR note, or a busy pattern, could silence the whole Digitone until a reboot), makes PLATEAU dependable, and adds **GLASS**, a new filter type after the Serge VCFQ, on every machine's FLTR page.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, SMEAR and PLATEAU tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- **THOLET is gone:** a saved THOLET track opens as FM Tone.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).


**Fixed in v0.18**
- **The silent unit.** A SMEAR note gave a short burst, then every machine went silent until a reboot. SMEAR's VOC reader needed more DSP time than the unit has: one VOC note ran the DSP past its frame, and that stops the audio. Fixed three ways:
  - **VOC is off for now.** BLND's VOC end plays the PLAY reader; PLAY and GRN work as before. A redesigned VOC that fits the unit comes in a later release.
  - **The time guard now expects a heavy voice's peak cost**, not just its last block's, so no voice can push the DSP past its frame.
  - **An output guard:** any machine's block that is not finite or runs away is replaced by silence, and that voice starts afresh. Nothing can poison the effects or the master any more.
- **PLATEAU did nothing when other tracks played.** When the DSP was busy, the time guard dropped PLATEAU's voice for the rest of the note. Now it only skips a busy block and comes back on the next.
- The gate now costs every machine on a model of the real unit (memory waits included), not just its instruction count. That is the check that would have caught this.

**New in v0.18**
- **GLASS**, a filter type after the Serge VCFQ (MACHINE SEL's FLTR list: **Glass**, after Equalizer), on every machine, stock or RIFT.
  - A state-variable filter like Multi-Mode, built to ring. At high RES a hard hit rings as a clean, round tone (a wine glass tapped with a screwdriver), never clipped or harsh, and the low end stays under the ring. It loves very short, popping sounds.
  - **RES** is ring time: from a few milliseconds to about four seconds, the same at every pitch. With KEY.T up, the ring follows your notes.
  - **TYPE** morphs low-pass → band-pass → high-pass.
  - **Its own envelope:** ATK and DEC, from each trig, with **ABND** and **DBND** (on SUS and REL) bending each segment: fully left exponential, middle straight, fully right logarithmic. ENV sets its depth on the cutoff.
  - **LPG** (page 2, in ENV DELAY's place, ON from 64): a low-pass gate. Level and cutoff open together at each trig and close like a vactrol; DEC sets how long. Bongo-like pings.
- **MACHINE SEL:** PLATEAU in a section of its own (the FX machines), with its own logo and **FX** under it; SMEAR first of the sample machines, with its own logo.
- Includes v0.17 (PLATEAU, the BUS, THOLET removed).

**What to test**
0. SMEAR on a track with a sample in SAMP: play notes from the [TRIG] keys for a while, BLND through its range: it keeps sounding and nothing else goes silent. PLATEAU with two other tracks playing: the reverb stays.
1. FLTR: Glass on a stock machine and on a RIFT machine. Turn RES up: short hits ring as clean tones.
2. TYPE through LP, BP, HP; FREQ and RES together.
3. KEY.T up, RES high: play a melody; the ring follows the notes.
4. ENV up; ABND and DBND fully left, then fully right: the sweep's shape changes.
5. LPG above 64: bongo-like pings; DEC sets their length.
6. Save with a GLASS track, power-cycle, load: still Glass.

**Known issues**
- SMEAR's VOC reader is off (see above).
- GLASS's layout is a first version: the bends sit on SUS and REL (no sustain or release on GLASS), and LPG is a knob (ON from 64), not a switch.
- A sound saved with GLASS loads as Multi-Mode on stock firmware or the DN2 mk2 mod.
- PLATEAU's voice can be taken by the voice allocator on a busy pattern (reserve one for its track).
