# v0.16 · SMEAR

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.16 adds **SMEAR**, a granular, phase-vocoded, stretchy looper modelled on the Qu-Bit Nebulae (in WERP's place among the sample machines), **eight new LIGATURE blocks**, and **machine logos in MACHINE SEL**.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, THOLET and SMEAR tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- **WERP is gone:** a saved WERP track opens as SMEAR, with odd settings.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.16**
- **SMEAR** (MACHINE SEL: Smear, in WERP's place). It plays a sample or a FLEX buffer through three readers of one loop:
  - **VOC** is a phase vocoder that stretches without changing pitch. SPD at 0 (FRZ) freezes the sound in place.
  - **PLAY** is a plain, tape-like read.
  - **GRN** is a cloud of grains.
  - **BLND** moves between them. A note restarts the loop at STRT and plays at its pitch, so SMEAR plays chromatically.
  - **Page 1:** TUNE, SPD (−4 to +4), BLND, SAMP / STRT, SIZE (the loop), DENS (grains a second), LEV.
  - **Page 2:** OVLP (grain length), SPRY (scatter), PRND (random detune), WIN (seven grain windows).
  - The two vocoders are shared by the whole instrument. The two newest SMEAR notes that use VOC get one each.
- **ORGAN, THOLET and SMEAR are silent for the first moment after power-on.** To make room for SMEAR in the DSP, their tables are no longer in the DSP's program. The Digitone's main processor sends them at power-on instead.
- **Eight new LIGATURE blocks**, from WEFT:
  - **TICK:** a clock at a division of the tempo (RATE), with CHANCE.
  - **ENV:** attack/decay, triggered by its input.
  - **VCA:** LEVEL and CURVE, from linear to exponential.
  - **CDIV:** a clock divider or multiplier.
  - **DUST:** random particles. **CHAOS:** a logistic map.
  - **CRUSH:** fewer bits and held samples.
  - **LISSA:** a Lissajous LFO, at nine X:Y ratios.

  Each block is explained, with examples, in the manual's LIGATURE section.
- **Machine logos in MACHINE SEL:** with the cursor on a machine, its logo replaces the stock picture.
  - FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT and LIGATURE each have their own.
  - DIGIDRUM has its own, and its label reads **DRUM**.
  - FLEX has its own, labelled **REC**.
  - Every other DT2 sample machine shows the DT2 logo, labelled **SAMP**.

  Stock machines and THOLET keep the stock picture for now.
- Includes v0.15 (THOLET, KEYBOARD HOLD).

**What to test**
1. MACHINE SEL: Smear where Werp was. Load a sample, then play: with BLND at 0 it's a stretched version of the sample.
2. Turn SPD to FRZ: the sound holds in place. Move STRT to scan through the sample.
3. Turn BLND up to 127, then turn DENS, OVLP and SPRY: a grain cloud. Change WIN.
4. Play two SMEAR tracks at once, then a third: all three sound. The oldest note's vocoder part gives way.
5. Power-cycle with ORGAN or THOLET on a track: within about a second after the start, they sound.
6. LIGATURE: in EDIT, set a block to TICK (RATE 1/16, CHANCE 64) into an ENV into a VCA on a WAVE: random rhythmic gating in time.
7. MACHINE SEL: move the cursor over FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, FLEX and ONESHOT: each logo (DRUM, REC, SAMP under the last three); FM TONE and the FLTR column: the stock pictures.

**Known issues**
- A MAIN or IN FLEX buffer saved to a sample slot plays 12 dB quieter than the buffer did.
- ERASE MOD DATA doesn't clear the tunings yet.

**Coming next (not available yet)**
- **v0.17:**
  - **BUS:** pick any tracks with the trig keys on a REC BUS page. FLEX can record their sum (SRC BUS).
  - **FX machines:** a track type fed by the BUS. The first effect is **PLATEAU**, a plate reverb after Dattorro's design (the one Valley's Plateau is built on), with SIZE, DIFFUSION, DECAY, damping, modulation, HOLD (freeze) and a TUNED mode.
  - **GLASS:** a new filter type after the Serge VCFQ. It rings glassy at high resonance without clipping, and keeps the low end. Page 2 gets ATTACK and DECAY BEND, and an **LPG** switch.
  - **THOLET will be removed.** A saved THOLET track will open as FM Tone, so keep that in mind for any THOLET sounds you make now.
  - DIGIDRUM gets a new logo.
- Later: more filter types (a morphing Z-plane-style filter, a loop-shaped SVF), more FX machines, and LIGATURE blocks with delay lines.
