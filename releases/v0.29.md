# v0.29 · MONO

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.29 continues the stability work: SMEAR becomes one voice a track, two reports from the bug channel are fixed, and the DSP gets a little more room.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** and **QUICK GUIDE** links sit beside the version list.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, SPALL) and the plate reverb will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.29**
- **SMEAR plays one voice a track.** A new note fades the sounding one out in under a millisecond and takes over, like a mono synth. Overlapping notes no longer pile up into SMEAR's drop-outs and crackle.
- **SMEAR's SIZE and STRT run 0.00 to 120.00**, like ONESHOT's. Before, SIZE went on to 127, and 121 to 127 read past the end of its table and gave odd loop lengths. A project saved with SIZE above 120 now plays the whole loop.
- **DIGIDRUM is dropped far less.** The DSP's time guard now expects what each drum type really costs. BD, SD BASIC and PC are reserved about half of what they were before, so a kick next to SPALL or other busy tracks is left out much less often.
- **The master reverb** keeps its short lines in faster memory: the same sound bit for bit, a little less DSP time.
- Includes v0.28 (RUBBER pulled, the sample machines and SMEAR under load, the shorter reverb tail, DIGIDRUM's names).

**What to test**
1. SMEAR: hold a note and play another on the same track. The first fades and the second takes over, with no click and no pile-up.
2. SMEAR SIZE: turn it all the way up. 120.00 is the whole loop, and there is nothing past it.
3. DIGIDRUM: a kick pattern next to SPALL, ORGAN or CONTOUR. Fewer drop-outs, and the CPU marker shows less often.

**Known issue**
- One report of FASCIA going silent after FDBK was raised and lowered, until the patch was cleared. It has not been reproduced yet. If it happens to you, note the patch's values and whether the CPU marker showed.
