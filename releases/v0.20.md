# v0.20 · NXT

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.20 brings over the DN2 mk2 mod's slide update, **SLEN NXT**, and fixes CONTOUR's feedback.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, PLATEAU) will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.20**
- **SLEN NXT**, the Analog Four's rule: a slide lasts until the track's next trig (a note or a lock trig), round the track's length; with no other trig, the whole length.
  - NXT is SLEN's first choice, and what a slide trig with no SLEN lock now does (before: 1 step). The fixed lengths (1/8 .. 64 steps) are as before.
  - Works on every machine's controls, RIFT's included.
  - Older projects: a slide trig with no SLEN lock now glides to the next trig. Lock SLEN to 1 to keep the old timing.
- **CONTOUR's FDBK rescaled:** past about 36 it was unusable; now the whole knob is the usable range, with the subtle low end spread wide.
- **CONTOUR stays in key on high notes** with FDBK and FM up. Up to B3 every note sounds exactly as before; above it, each note keeps B3's character transposed instead of drifting off the note. A high CONTOUR note with feedback costs the DSP more (up to about four times).
- Includes v0.19 (CONTOUR, the stock-size pages, SMEAR's display).

**What to test**
1. A p-lock with SLD on and no SLEN lock, trigs on steps 1 and 5: the glide takes the four steps.
2. One trig on the track: the slide takes the whole pattern.
3. SLEN 1: one step, as before.
4. A per-track length: NXT wraps round that track's length.
5. CONTOUR with FDBK and FM up, from C2 to C6: in key all the way up.
6. FDBK from 0 to 127: usable across the whole knob.

**Known issues**
