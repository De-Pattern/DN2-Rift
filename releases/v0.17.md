# v0.17 · PLATEAU

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.17 adds **PLATEAU**, RIFT's first **FX machine** (a plate reverb on its own track, fed by a BUS of the tracks you choose), the **BUS** for FLEX (record the sum of chosen tracks), and removes THOLET.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, SMEAR and PLATEAU tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- **THOLET is gone:** a saved THOLET track opens as FM Tone.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.17**
- **PLATEAU**, an FX machine (MACHINE SEL: Plateau, after DIGIDRUM). A plate reverb after Jon Dattorro's design, the one Valley's Plateau is built on.
  - It reverberates the tracks on its **BUS**; it makes no sound of its own. Its output is the track's voice (mono), so the track's filter, amp, LFOs, sends, LEVEL and p-locks all work on the reverb. The tracks you feed it keep their own dry sound.
  - **To use it:** give the PLATEAU track a long note (an INF trig, or KEYBOARD HOLD). The reverb runs while that note sounds. Reserve a voice for the track in the voice allocation menu so a busy pattern can't take it.
  - **The BUS page:** [FUNC] + [VOICE] on the PLATEAU track. Sixteen cells, one per track: [TRIG n] puts track n in or out, its LED lit while it's in. YES or NO goes back. It starts with every track except FLEX and FX tracks; its own track can never be in.
  - **Page 1:** PRED, SIZE, DCAY, DIFF / DAMP, BAND (input high cut), LCUT (input low cut), LEV.
  - **Page 2:** TLOW (tank low cut), MSPD, MDEP (modulation) / HOLD (freeze), TUND (the plate rings at the note's pitch), IGN (input gain), DRV (a soft drive inside the tank), ERLY (early reflections).
  - One PLATEAU plays at a time: the newest to start takes over, an older one goes quiet. A new one is silent for about 15 ms while it clears its memory.
- **FLEX: SRC BUS.** Record the sum of chosen tracks. Pick them on **REC BUS**, FLEX REC's third page (RIGHT from REC SETUP), with the [TRIG] keys. MUTE SRC mutes every track on the bus. A loud bus can clip: turn GAIN down.
- **THOLET is removed.**
- DIGIDRUM's MACHINE SEL logo is now a drum hit.
- Includes v0.16 (SMEAR, the LIGATURE blocks, the logos).

**What to test**
1. MACHINE SEL: Plateau on a free track, with an INF trig; play two other tracks.
2. [FUNC] + [VOICE] on it: the BUS page. Take a playing track out and back in with its [TRIG] key: the reverb follows.
3. Turn SIZE, DCAY, DIFF, DAMP. HOLD ON: the tail freezes; OFF: it fades.
4. TUND ON, then play notes on the PLATEAU track: the plate rings at each note.
5. A second PLATEAU track triggered: it takes over, the first goes quiet.
6. FLEX: REC SETUP, RIGHT to REC BUS. Pick two tracks, set SRC to BUS and record a take: both tracks are in it.
7. Open a project with a THOLET track: it's FM Tone now.

**Known issues**
- PLATEAU's voice can be taken by the voice allocator on a busy pattern (reserve one for its track).
- A MAIN or IN FLEX buffer saved to a sample slot plays 12 dB quieter than the buffer did.
- ERASE MOD DATA doesn't clear the tunings yet.

**Coming next (not available yet)**
- **GLASS**, a new filter type after the Serge VCFQ, on every machine's FLTR page: it rings glassy at high resonance without clipping and keeps the low end. Page 2 gets ATTACK and DECAY BEND, and an **LPG** switch.
- More FX machines: an all-pass network, a formant bank, a triple resonator with DUST.
- More filter types (a morphing Z-plane-style filter, a loop-shaped SVF), and better icons for every machine.
