# v0.14 · MONTUNE

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.14 brings over two more of the DN2 mk2 mod's updates, MON on the RECORDER and MICROTUNING, and fixes FLEX takes of MAIN and IN being far too quiet.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE and DIGIDRUM tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.14**
- **FLEX takes of MAIN and IN play 12 dB louder** (thanks Error Kit). They were far quieter than their source, and turning GAIN up to make up for it clipped the take. Now the take is recorded as it comes in, never pushed into the clip, and played back 12 dB up. Takes of a single track are unchanged (they already match their source).
- **MON on the RECORDER** (from the DN2 mk2 mod, niko_24956's request): knob C turns MON on or off. With SRC IN, you hear the input through the main out while the recorder is armed or recording, at the level it records. "MON" shows in the box's corner while it is on. It starts OFF and isn't saved.
- **MICROTUNING** (from the DN2 mk2 mod): one tuning per project, and each of the 16 tracks tuned or not.
  - 11 factory tunings (12-TET, Just Major, Just Minor, Pythagorean, Meantone 1/4, Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti, Arabic Rast, Poole Rod12, Poole12 Min) and USER 1..8.
  - KB SETUP's empty H slot is **TUNE**: turn it to set the track's tuning, push it to open TUNING (A TUNE, B ROOT, C TRK, D ALL). YES on a USER tuning opens EDIT (A NOTE, B CENT, C .1CT, D FROM; FUNC + YES copies FROM in).
  - Everything a tuned track plays is retuned: keyboard, trigs, chords, arp and slides, on every RIFT machine. MIDI tracks and MIDI out stay 12-TET; SLICE and GRID tracks are never retuned.
  - Saved with the project.

**What to test**
1. FLEX from MAIN, GAIN 0: about as loud as you heard it, no distortion. The same from IN with the input up.
2. FLEX from a single track: as before (as loud as its source).
3. RECORDER, SRC IN, knob C to MON ON: you hear the input while armed and recording; "MON" in the corner.
4. KB SETUP: turn H to a tuning; play: the track is retuned. Push H: TUNING; set ROOT; C TRK off: back to 12-TET.
5. Save, load another project, load back, power-cycle: the tuning is kept.

**Known issues**
- A MAIN or IN FLEX buffer saved to a sample slot plays 12 dB quieter than the buffer did (the save keeps the take as recorded).
- ERASE MOD DATA doesn't clear the tunings yet.
