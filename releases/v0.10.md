# v0.10 · DIGIDRUM

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. It drops the Machinedrum and Monomachine machines and puts new engines in their place. v0.10 adds **DIGIDRUM**, digital drums after the Syntakt's.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **From v0.8:** a RESONATOR track that was never re-saved in v0.9 now opens as **DIGIDRUM**, with whatever settings its old values give. Change that track's machine.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE and DIGIDRUM tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.10**
- **DIGIDRUM**, in MACHINE SEL after LIGATURE: six digital drum voices after the Syntakt's: **BD Modern, SD Basic, SD Vintage, CP Vintage, CY Alloy, PC Carbon**.
- **TYPE** picks the voice, and a trig can p-lock it: one track can play a kick, a snare and a clap in one pattern.
- Page 1 (DRUM): TYPE, TUNE, PNCH, DEC, then four controls named by the TYPE (each voice has its own).
- Page 2 (TONE), Machinedrum-style: OVER (overdrive), EQF/EQG (a peaking EQ), AMD/AMF (a tremolo), FLTF/FLTW (a base/width filter) and LEV.
- On the LINE pages, like every RIFT machine.
- The other engines are unchanged from v0.9.

**What to test**
1. Pick DIGIDRUM in MACHINE SEL; play each TYPE.
2. P-lock TYPE on a few trigs: each trig plays its own voice.
3. Turn TYPE: the last four page-1 labels change with it.
4. Page 2: EQ, tremolo, filter and level each do what they say.
5. Play it hard with other tracks running: no clicks or dropouts beyond what FASCIA's heavy voices already cause.
