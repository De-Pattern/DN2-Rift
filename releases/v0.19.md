# v0.19 · CONTOUR

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.19 adds **CONTOUR**, a wave-terrain synth, redraws every RIFT machine page at stock's size, and gives SMEAR a granular display.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page and control.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks and the unit's cost model, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT: their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about.
- **Going back:** RIFT's machines (FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE, DIGIDRUM, CONTOUR, SMEAR, PLATEAU) will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only.

**New in v0.19**
- **CONTOUR**, a wave-terrain synth (MACHINE SEL: Contour, after DIGIDRUM). A point orbits over a surface; its height is the sound.
  - **Page 1, ORBIT:** TUNE, RATIO (Y's speed against X's: musical ratios, then a beating one), SIZX, SIZY / CNTX, CNTY (where the orbit sits), ROT, DRFT (a slow wander).
  - **Page 2, LAND:** SURF (SINE, FOLD, CHEB, FM, RING, EDGE, CHAOS, BITS), MORPH (into the next surface), WARP, FOLD / FM, FDBK, GRIT, LEV.
  - The idea comes from TERRAIN for the Digitone II (ycmdynp9vw-coder); CONTOUR is RIFT's own code.
- **Bigger machine pages:** every RIFT machine's icons at stock's size, its labels in stock's font, the two rows down to the foot of the screen as stock's pages are.
- **SMEAR's page 2 shows the sound:** the sample's waveform, the loop, SMEAR's own read point, and a cloud of grains round it (DENS how many, SPRY how far, OVLP how long).
- Includes v0.18 (the silent-unit fix, PLATEAU dependable, GLASS).

**What to test**
1. MACHINE SEL: Contour, after Digidrum. Play notes; turn SURF through its eight surfaces, then MORPH.
2. SIZX and SIZY from small to large; CNTX and CNTY off the centre; ROT; RATIO round its range.
3. FM, FDBK, FOLD and GRIT up together: it stays stable and nothing else goes silent.
4. Four or five CONTOUR tracks with FASCIA and ORGAN playing: no dropouts.
5. Every RIFT machine's pages: the icons bigger, the labels in stock's font, nothing overlapping.
6. SMEAR with a sample: page 2 shows the waveform, the read point moving, the grain cloud following DENS, SPRY and OVLP.

**Known issues**
- CONTOUR has no MACHINE SEL logo yet (stock's picture shows).
- The bigger icons are the old ones scaled up: some fine dots are uneven until the redrawn set.
- SMEAR's grain cloud is drawn from the knobs, not the DSP's own grains: it moves with the read point and answers every knob, but is not grain for grain.
- SMEAR's VOC is still off.
