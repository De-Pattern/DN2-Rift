# v0.13 · PARITY

**RIFT** is a second firmware for the Digitone II, separate from the DN2 mk2 mod. v0.13 brings over the DN2 mk2 mod's latest sampler updates, so the two firmwares are on par again: the slice editor, GRID's ALLOCATE fix, and FLEX takes at the level you hear.

**Build it:** the DN2 Mod Patcher, its RIFT section: https://de-pattern.github.io/dn2mk2/patcher.html#rift. It builds RIFT in your browser from your own Digitone II OS 1.11, Digitakt II OS 1.17 and Machinedrum UW OS 1.63 files, and only lets you save it if it is byte for byte the cleared file. The **MANUAL** button beside the version list explains every page.

**⚠ Before you flash**
- **RIFT is new and early.** It passed the full ship gate (both processors' emulators, the DSP checks, boot, save and load), but it has had very little time on real instruments yet. Expect rough edges.
- **Back up** the +Drive and your projects with Elektron Transfer first, every time. Keep the stock OS 1.11 file and a DIN MIDI cable for recovery. The start-up code and the updater are Elektron's own, untouched.
- **Coming from the DN2 mk2 mod:** flash it like any mod version. Tracks with **Perc** or the **Monomachine** machines open as **FM Tone** in RIFT (as stock opens them): their sounds are not kept. Save copies of projects you care about and start RIFT work in new projects.
- **Going back:** FASCIA, ORGAN, SILT, LIGATURE and DIGIDRUM tracks are RIFT's own and will not sound the same, or at all, on the mod or stock. Keep RIFT projects apart.
- OS 1.11 only. On 1.12 or later, flashing is a downgrade (see the patcher).

**New in v0.13** (from the DN2 mk2 mod's v0.60, v0.62 and v0.63)
- **EDIT SLICE POINTS** (impbox's requests):
  - edits play straight away: move START or END, stop turning, play the slice's key and you hear it, no YES needed (YES and NO still close the editor);
  - playing a slice selects it, from the keyboard, the trig keys or the sequencer;
  - ZOOM goes down to 128 frames, small enough to see single cycles;
  - FUNC + E (START) or FUNC + H (END) snaps the point to the nearest zero crossing.
- **CREATE SLICE GRID:** FUNC + E jumps SLICES 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64, a detent a jump; E alone still moves one (thanks Wolljamin, who asked while using RIFT).
- **GRID's ALLOCATE SLICE:** NO now leaves the menu and keeps the locks RANDOM made, as on the Digitakt II (before, NO put the old locks back, so RANDOM seemed to do nothing).
- **FLEX level:** a FLEX take of a single track (SRC TRK) now plays back about as loud as the track was in the mix, with GAIN at 0. **Heads up:** if you had turned GAIN up on a FLEX track, it is now 24 dB hotter; set GAIN back to 0. MAIN and IN takes are unchanged for now.
- **FLEX save:** saving a FLEX buffer to a slot no longer normalises it (it used to normalise the take the FLEX track was playing, so it jumped to full scale). SAMPLING and other recordings are still normalised as before.

**What to test**
1. Move a slice's START, stop turning, play its key: you hear the change without YES.
2. Play the track: the editor selects the slice that plays.
3. ZOOM all the way in: single cycles visible. FUNC + E / FUNC + H land on zero crossings.
4. CREATE SLICE GRID: FUNC + E steps 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 / 64.
5. GRID, ALLOCATE SLICE, RANDOM, then NO: RANDOM's locks stay.
6. FLEX from a single track, GAIN 0: the take is about as loud as the track was.
7. Save the FLEX buffer while it plays: no level jump, and the saved sample loads at the same level.

**Known issues**
- Playing the same note again on the same voice doesn't re-select its slice (a different note does).
- MAIN and IN FLEX takes are still on the quiet side.
